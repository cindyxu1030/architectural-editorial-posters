# Ratio playbook

Use `R = width / height`.

| Ratio family | Range | Reading path | Best carrier | Adaptation priority |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| Narrow portrait | `R < 0.67` | top to bottom | tower, atrium, stacked facade, vertical street canyon | stack modules; use one vertical path; avoid wide titles unless rotated or broken into lines |
| Portrait | `0.67 <= R < 0.85` | vertical with diagonal interruption | facade, stairs, cylindrical bays, ramp | keep title in upper third; let one motif travel through the middle |
| Near square | `0.85 <= R < 1.20` | center-out or Z path | courtyard, corner, portal, clock, circular instrument | use a central anchor; distribute modules around it without making a dashboard |
| Landscape | `1.20 <= R < 1.80` | left to right or diagonal | long facade, bridge, roofline, street edge | use horizontal title bands; reserve one side for information |
| Ultrawide | `R >= 1.80` | cinematic left to right | skyline, long corridor, repeated facade | use strong edge anchors; avoid a tiny centered subject; stretch the path, not the type |

## Safe zones

Start with an inset of 5 to 7 percent of the shorter edge. Increase it when platform controls, printing trim, or a known crop require more room. Keep logos, dates, and calls to action within the safe zone. Decorative lines and cropped title shapes may cross it.

## Scaling rules

- Scale type from the shorter edge, then tune optically.
- Keep the primary title large enough to survive a 25 percent thumbnail preview.
- Preserve negative space as an active module. Do not fill extra width or height with more decoration.
- Let the architectural crop change between ratios. The visual subject may move.
- Keep the same accent color and hierarchy across a campaign set.

## Reflow by family

### Portrait to landscape

- change a stacked title into one or two horizontal lines
- shift the architectural carrier from vertical stack to horizontal run
- turn a vertical connector path into a long curve or segmented baseline
- move the information cluster from bottom to a side rail

### Landscape to portrait

- break the title into short lines
- choose a taller crop or regenerate with vertical architecture
- replace a side rail with top and bottom modules
- use one vertical accent path instead of several horizontal rules

### Any ratio to square

- find one central anchor such as a void, window, clock, portal, or circular diagram
- wrap secondary information around the anchor
- keep at least one asymmetrical edge crop to avoid a static badge composition

## Multi-format production order

1. List every required format and its protected content.
2. Choose the hardest frame: the narrowest portrait or widest landscape.
3. Lock concept, carrier, hierarchy, palette, and type family.
4. Build each ratio as a separate composition using the same system.
5. Compare the set at thumbnail size for shared identity.
6. Verify each export independently. Never assume one correct ratio proves the others.
